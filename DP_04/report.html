Architectural Guardrail: The Inherent Boundaries of LVM Snapshots

LVM snapshots provide exceptional near-zero RTO for logical failures and accidental deletions. However, snapshots are not independent backups:

  • Origin Co-dependency: Classic copy-on-write snapshots store only modified delta blocks. Total failure or deletion of the origin volume destroys all child snapshots instantly.
  • Storage Saturation Invalidation: An unmonitored snapshot that reaches 100% capacity becomes permanently invalid (attribute I) and cannot be recovered.
  • Offline Rollback Requirement: Full rollback via lvconvert --merge alters the block allocation table directly and requires unmounting the origin filesystem to prevent metadata corruption.
  • True Disaster Protection: Disaster recovery across hardware failures or volume group loss requires immutable archive backups exported outside the volume group (as proven in Scenario 5).
